Burn all burner numbers

Lost your phone? Enter your handle and the emergency burn code you wrote down. Every burner number on the account stops working at once and its calls and texts are deleted from the relay. You can't undo this, and the code works only once.

Your main number and your chats are not touched. To cut off the lost phone itself, remove it under Settings → Devices on another device, or restore from your backup.
